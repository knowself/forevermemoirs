# Offer Ladder — ForeverMemoirs 2.0

Revival of ForeverMemoirs LLC (Hermosa Beach, CA). Original business (2020–2021, archived): A&E Biography-style personal documentary films, $1,375–$9,495, film crew, DVDs, LA area only.

## 2.0 tiers

**$49 — Memory Rescue.** Single photo restoration, 48-hour turnaround. Front door; TikTok before/afters bring customers in.

**$149 — The Shoebox.** 5 photos restored + a 60-second video tribute with original music. Where the photo lab meets the jingle factory.

**$997 — The Memoir Film.** Remote interviews over Zoom, AI-assisted editing, restored photos woven in. The old $2,750 package rebuilt without the film crew.

**$2,997 — The Biography.** Multi-session, family interviews, full archival treatment. The old $9,495 flagship at a third of the price.

**$297/yr — LifeCharts 2.0.** Families send videos all year; we cut the annual film. The old $625/yr child's video diary, modernized.

## Economics

- Compute cost per photo: ~$0 (local open-source models, CPU).
- Real cost: ~5 minutes human QC per photo.
- At $49/photo: ~95% margin. 10 photos/day ≈ $15k/mo revenue potential.

## Brand

- Tagline: "No Life Story Should Go Untold."
- Privacy pledge (carried from 1.0): the films belong to the client; use/re-broadcast controlled by their written consent.
