# Contractor Confidentiality Agreement

**Between:** ForeverMemoirs ("Company")
**And:** ______________________________ ("Contractor")
**Date:** ______________________________

## 1. Why this agreement exists

Contractor will perform photo restoration, video editing, or related production
work for Company. That work requires access to customers' family photographs,
films, names, and contact information — some of the most personal material a
family owns. This agreement protects those families.

## 2. What is confidential

"Confidential Information" means all customer photographs (originals and
restorations), films and edits, customer names and contact details, order
information, and any Company methods or processes Contractor learns during
the engagement.

## 3. Contractor's obligations

Contractor agrees to:

- Use Confidential Information only to perform the assigned work.
- Never copy, keep, or use customer photographs or films for any other purpose —
  including portfolio, social media, or AI model training — without the
  customer's separate written permission obtained through Company.
- Store all work files securely, never on shared or public systems, and return
  or verifiably delete all copies when the engagement ends or when Company asks.
- Tell Company immediately if Confidential Information is lost, stolen, or
  accessed by anyone unauthorized.

## 4. What is not confidential

Information that is already public, that Contractor knew before this engagement
(without breaking any confidence), or that Contractor is required to disclose
by law (in which case Contractor tells Company first, so Company can respond).

## 5. Term

These obligations survive the end of the engagement. Customer photographs are
confidential forever — there is no expiry on a family's privacy.

## 6. Remedies

Contractor understands that a breach may cause harm that money alone cannot
fix, and Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies.

## 7. Governing law

The laws of the State of California.
